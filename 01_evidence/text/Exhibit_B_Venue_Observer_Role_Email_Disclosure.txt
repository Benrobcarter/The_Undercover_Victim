Exhibit B
Venue Observer Role & Email Disclosure — SAR Addendum

This annex forms part of my Subject Access Request (SAR) to Brighton Fringe Ltd.

It specifically concerns my role as Venues’ Observer and the unlawful disclosure of my personal
email address during the festival.

Scope of Addendum:
1. Appointment & Incentives:

- All communications, board papers, and notices concerning my appointment.
- Any incentives offered for the role (governance access, recognition, etc.).

2. Charity Obligations & Risk Management:

- Trustee papers, governance notes, or policies linking the role to obligations under the Charities
Act 2011, including risk management, safeguarding, stakeholder representation, transparency, and

accountability.
3. Board Minutes Praising My Work:
- Minutes or trustee communications congratulating me for attracting venues.

- Records noting | had ‘already attracted four new venues and expanded reach’.
4. Unlawful Sharing of Personal Email:

- Communications, internal discussions, or distribution lists where my personal email
(benrobcarter@gmail.com) was circulated alongside chair@brightonfringe.org.
- Justifications considered, audit trails of who authorised this circulation.

Legal & Governance Context:
- UK GDPR/DPA 2018: Articles 5(1)(a), 6, and 15 — breached via unlawful disclosure.

- Charities Act 2011 & CC9: Trustees’ duty to manage risk and act transparently.
Escalation:
- Any delay or excuse will be forwarded to the Charity Commission.
- Non-compliance will also be escalated to the ICO as a GDPR breach.

Submitted by:
Ben Carter, Director — Half a Camel Productions Ltd. (Venue Manager/Owner — Half a Camel
