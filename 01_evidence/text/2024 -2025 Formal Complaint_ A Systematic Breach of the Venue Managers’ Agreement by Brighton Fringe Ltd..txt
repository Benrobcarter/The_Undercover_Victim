Introduction
This document outlines a formal complaint against Brighton Fringe Ltd. (“the Fringe’) for a
systematic, escalating, and multifaceted breach of every material clause of our Venue
Managers’ Agreement. The evidence proves that the actions of Brighton Fringe, spanning
from a premeditated, financially motivated strategy in 2023 to the operational collapse of
2024 and the hostile takeover of my company, Half a Camel Productions, in 2025, were not
isolated failures but a calculated campaign.
This campaign violated the good faith partnership required by our contract, culminating in the
destruction of my business, reputation, and health. Facing a financial crisis of their own
making, Brighton Fringe identified my successful and expanding operation as a commercial

asset to be seized. Their subsequent claims of a "safeguarding intervention" were a cynical
and malicious pretext to justify that hostile takeover, a fact proven by their own internal
documents, their pattern of behaviour, and testimony from third parties.
