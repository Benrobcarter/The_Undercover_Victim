Brighton Fringe 2025 has had its best day of ticket sales since before the

pandemic.

The festival, which launched on May 2 and runs until June 1, is England's biggest

arts festival and attracts hundreds of thousands of visitors, including artists from
all over the world.

This year, the programme includes more than 800 shows in more than 160
venues, with thousands of performances across a wide range of genres,
including dance, theatre, comedy, spoken word, circus, and cabaret.

Amy Keogh, managing director of Brighton Fringe, said: "There’s never a better
time to visit Brighton than during the Fringe.
"The city is buzzing every day and there is a packed programme of shows and

events, from the cultured to the weird or downright silly.

"It's exciting knowing you can be among the first to preview the latest work of an
internationally well-known comedian, or talented new artists waiting to be

discovered.

"Whenever our team walks around Brighton, from venue to venue, it’s a pleasure

seeing so many people having a good time.
"We've just had the best day of ticket sales since pre-Covid years.
"Although there are lots of events to choose from, and some larger venues do
run a few of their shows every day, I'd urge you to get your tickets soon because

some shows are already sold out, and some are one-offs, happening ona
specific date only.
"We don’t want you to miss out."

Duncan Lustig-Prean, chair of the board of Brighton Fringe, said: "As well as
variety, we are passionate about inclusion.
"Our 'open access' festival format is affordable for all artists.
“Many events are free to attend, some are 'Pay What You Can’, and all those are
offset by ticket sales, with average ticket prices under £10.
"Brighton Fringe receives no public funding - and we add much value to the
community.

"With 30 per cent of all artists coming from Brighton and Hove, we want local

people to get behind the Fringe and feel proud of hosting such an important
performing arts festival."

Tickets for every show and event are available to book now via
brightonfringe.org or 01273 917 272.

The box office is open every day from 10am until 59m at The Theatre Royal, New
Road, BN1 1SD.
