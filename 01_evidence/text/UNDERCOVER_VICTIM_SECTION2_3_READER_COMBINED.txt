2.0 — The Core Complaint & Timeline of Failures

“This complaint is built upon a body of evidence whose quality is a direct result of systemic failure.”
— Ben Carter

Safeguarding was breached. Evidence was mishandled. Suicide risk went unlogged. Misconduct
became minimisation. This section sets out the chronology of those failures, and the contradictions
they produced.

2.1 — July Call and the Deletion Log

“Where they chose silence, | created record.” — Ben Carter
July 2024 — my first submission. By November, a log note recorded that the key audio had been
‘listened to & deleted.’

A record of risk was treated as disposable. Audit trails evaporated. Safeguarding turned into
silence.

2.2 — Bail Confirmation Mishandled

“Feeling | had ‘taken on the fucking police and won,’ | escalated my case to the IOPC.” — Ben

Carter
February—March 2025 — during the bail confirmation period, my abuser was forwarded unredacted

material. Sussex Police turned safeguarding into exposure, GDPR into refusal.
At the same time, they visited my flat without BWV. My safety was paper-thin, their records
incomplete.

2.3 — Procedural Inversion & NFA to Suspect

“POLICE PLEASE — not a plea, a prophecy.” — Ben Carter
22 April 2025: lawful conduct reframed as suspect. Five days later, on 27 April, an NFA-style

message was sent to the suspect before any safeguarding meeting.
The victim was still waiting for protection; the suspect received closure. This inversion stands as

proof of bias and premature communication.

2.4 — Suicide Attempt and SAR Refusal

“The system did not just fail to protect me; it created the conditions that required this extraordinary
level of selfladvocacy to simply survive.”

— Ben Carter

April 2025 — my suicide attempt was not logged. By June, when | filed a Subject Access Request

to test the system, it was refused after case closure.
The denial confirmed the pattern: disclosures erased, data blocked, survival weaponised against
me.

2.5 — Brighton Fringe Retaliation

“One big apple rotting from its core.” — Ben Carter
Brighton Fringe received safeguarding disclosures in May—June 2025. They withheld their minutes,
erased my name, and rewrote the record.
Where law required governance, they chose reputation. Where safety required disclosure, they
chose silence.
