On Mon, 7 Aug 2023 at 13:40, Duncan Lustig-Prean <chair@brightonfringe.org> wrote:

Dear Ben
| hope all is well.

As both a venue manager and their representative | wanted to let you know what
changes we are planning to make to the Venues Agreement and | also want to seek

your help in your Board Observer hat to expand our network of venues next year.
We are enhancing the venues agreement next year codifying the 15% discount for 15 or
more registrations and also increasing the allocation of tickets to 50/50 with the free
inclusion of click through button on Eventotron if the Fringe sells out.

One additional exciting benefit for venues and artists in the new agreement is our
undertaking to make the first 50% settlement payments after two weeks and weekly
thereafter. The final 50% will be paid within six weeks of the end of the Fringe but more
likely within for which will be our aim. | am sure that you will agree that this latter change

is of enormous benefit to our artists and to venues for their cashflow.
The change to 50% allocation was necessitated because one venue reneged on the

‘gentlemen's agreement which has always previously worked well (and reciprocally) for
all parties and declined on most occasions to increase the allocation from 30% when we
were sold out. This really hurt our artists financially and such actions reduce Fringe

income significantly and threaten our business model. We could not allow this t0

become a precedent.
| attach below this email the text with changes highlighted.

While we are not yet certain it is likely that Spiegeltent will not take part in the Fringe
next year. Adrian has told a couple of the team that he will not be coming. It is also likely
that we will not run Caravanserai next year. While there are tentative ideas to bring in
another venue run by Head First Acrobats this will not compensate for the reduction in
venue capacity such reductions will cause.
It would be very helpful if you could have a think about untapped venues in the city
whom you or the team could approach so that we can increase the number of venues
and our offering for audiences. We are a

little too centred on a small area of the city. Are

there some small venues, clubs, pubs, studios who you think we or you can approach to
bring into the Fringe? Do let me know what you think.
Best wishes

Duncan

The following amendments are intended:
"Venues undertake:

Fringe box office, including concession-priced tickets. This includes all ticket types such as family
tickets, personal assistant ticket etc

Brighton Fringe undertakes:

To pay out final box office settlements no later than 6 weeks after the end of the festival.
