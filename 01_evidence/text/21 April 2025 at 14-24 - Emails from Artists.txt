Hi Ben,
Thank you for your email.
The process from a registration being finalised to going online has always been a two-step
process.

The main reason for this is we need to proof-read events before they go online to ensure

that the information supplied is correct. Registrations frequently have incorrect age
restrictions, formatting, grammatical errors and typos, missing information etc. | have to
highlight issues such as this every week when proofing events, so it is a very important
step.

We want to ensure that artists listings go up as polished as possible to give them the best
representation of their event to audiences, which is why we raise any issues that have
arisen to the artist so they can make edits. This is also from the perspective of ensuring
there is accurate audience information so that people are not turned away on the door,
such as an event at an 18+ venue being listed as 14+.
This also has to be a manual process as any tickets that are not standard or concession
that an artist has requested have to be added by us - | can guarantee there is no
automated avenue for this.
We try to manually update all listings once per week, we do not have an auto update

feature due to the reasons listed above as we need to ensure added information Is
accurate.
