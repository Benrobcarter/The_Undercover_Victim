Help us improve
Our online complaints service is currently under review. Your feedback will help us improve it.
Please take ten minutes to tell us about your experience of using our online form:

What’s next
Timescales

We're supporting a high number of people with their complaints at the moment and it is taking
around 16 weeks to assign new complaints. We apologise for this delay but we want to reassure
you we take your complaint very seriously.
Communication

We will contact you about your complaint via email or phone. Please contact us to let us know if
you need us to communicate with you in a specific or alternative way: https://ico.org.uk/contactus. We will do whatever we can to support your needs.
Complaints process

One of our case officers will look into your complaint.
The case officer will:

e
e
e

weigh up the facts of what’s happened, fairly and impartially;
ask you and the organisation for further information, if they think they need it;
and
tell you the outcome.

There are a number of possible outcomes to your complaint, the most common are:

e

We tell the organisation to take action. We can tell the organisation to do
more to help resolve your complaint, explain their decision to you more clearly
or to make changes so similar issues don’t happen to you or other people in the
future.

e

We don’t take action. Despite the difficult experience you’ve had with the
organisation, it might be that they have followed the law - this means we’re
unable to take any action. There might be times when we find the organisation
was at fault but we’re happy with how they handled your concerns after that
and we chose not to take action.

You can find more information about the complaints process, potential outcomes and what to do if
you are unhappy with how we handle your complaint on our website: https://ico.org.uk/about-theico/our-information/our-service-standards/.
For information about what we do with personal data please see our privacy notice:
