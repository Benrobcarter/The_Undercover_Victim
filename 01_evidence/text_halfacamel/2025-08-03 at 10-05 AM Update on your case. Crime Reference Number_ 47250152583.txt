Victim support services

Need help after a crime? Victim support services (VSS) offer independent, free, confidential support.
Please click on the link for Safe Space Sussex for further information.

Victim needs assessment

As part of our commitment to supporting you, your Investigating Officer will talk with you about any
specific needs you may have, particularly those that could help how we investigate your case. This is
known as a Victim Needs Assessment. More information can be found here HERE.
It's important that you feel able to share any concerns or changing needs at any point during the
investigation. You can do this by speaking directly with your Investigating Officer, or via the Sussex
Police Portal where you'll be guided through how to share your needs with us.

Victim personal statements
Being a victim of crime can affect you in different ways, be it emotionally, physically, mentally or
financially.

A victim personal statement (VPS) gives you the opportunity to explain in your own words the impact the
crime has had on you and your family.
It will be taken into account by all criminal justice agencies involved in the case and it can play a key part
in sentencing. If you wish to make a Victim Personal Statement, please contact the investigator.
