A 500 seat circus tent has popped up in central Brighton for this year’s
Brighton Fringe.

The Circus Yard’s Big Puck tent at The Level is hosting a impressive selection of
physical theatre, and other jaw-dropping live performances across the next
month.

It will host three in-house produced shows and 29 visiting productions, making
for 32 different shows in total.

The line-up features three original productions from Revel Puck Circus, including
Nose Dive Assembly, a “gravity-defying” spectacle, The Ruckus, featuring
acrobatics and aerial skills, and The Ugly Duckling: A Circus Story.

Audiences will also be able to watch visiting productions including drag
showcases, cabaret and circus theatre.
The circular stage is 13 metres wide with 10 metres of headroom and has been
reinforced to support aerial work and high-energy movement.
Also on site is Little Puck, a smaller 200-seat tent.

Nearby is also the PucksBar, a covered space with room for around 200 people
and is open to all.
Visitors can expect a family-friendly bar, a range of food vendors, and

comfortable spots to sit and take a break.
Within the bar area there is also a tent with pedestal heaters that is open to the

public.
The Circus Yard will be open until June 1 2025.
Brighton Fringe, which is a registered charity, relies on grants and private

donations to continue hosting the festival every year.
For the first time, Brighton Fringe will receive funding from Arts Council England

to support the Fringe Academy - a series of workshops and talks to help
emerging artists and producers to develop their careers by providing free skillsbased training, advice and support.
The first Fringe Academy event will be a networking day, The Junction, on

Saturday, May 10, featuring a panel of experts from festivals around the UK
discussing collaboration, connections and partnerships.

Tickets for the acts who performed on the night - plus many, many more
amazing shows and events - are available to book now from brightonfringe.org
or 01273 917 272.
