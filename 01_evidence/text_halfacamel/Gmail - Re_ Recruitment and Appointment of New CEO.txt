Subject: Recruitment and Appointment of New CEO

Good morning,
You are all now aware of Julian's departure after this year's Fringe ends with his last
day being 20 June. This will allow Julian to oversee the take-down of Caravanserai
and other Fringe infrastructure, evaluations and to prepare for the critical task of box
office settlements. Senior staff have now been informed and other staff will be
informed early next week. Our observers have now also been informed. We will also
be informing our Council and other partners and the major venues next week. A press
release has been prepared for immediate release once the news leaks out as it
inevitably will. A ‘Fringe toned' announcement is being prepared for our social media.

Please keep Julian's departure to yourself until all we need to tell have been informed.

Having discussed the way ahead with Jamie and Julian, the intention is to have a short
‘interregnum’ in order to prevent overloading the Board and senior management team

by recruitment occurring during the crucial period of preparation and running of the
next Fringe when all our energies must be focussed on successful delivery, the new

venue, nurturing potential sponsors and funders and rapid response to ensure that we
maximise income generation proactively. This will also save funds as CEO pay will
pause for at least four months. It also enables us to reduce headcount should we need

to take the unpalatable decision to liquidate Brighton Fringe Operations Ltd or, god
forbid, the charity, were the season to fail.
Job advertisements are currently being drafted for Board approval and will be placed
immediately after the end of the Fringe with a view to initial interviews in early July.
With notice periods possibly of up to three months it is therefore possible that we do
not have a CEO in place until November. In the interim | propose that | act as
Executive Chair (unpaid) fulfilling the role of CEO with the support of Amy Keogh.
While saving significant salary costs, the disadvantage of this delay is that it may be

November before a new CEO will be in place. This limits the new CEO's impact in
shaping 2024's Fringe.
lan regularly visits the office and | am in most days. We have a hands-on approach
with financial and budgetary management and operational delivery. We have also

been tightening our governance systems. Having spent much time alongside Julian
and Amy Keogh | believe that with the support of lan and the strong knowledge and
abilities of Amy | will have adequate support to fill the gap on a temporary basis.
After sifting applications, | propose that the Board select up to four or five applicants

for initial interview by the Executive Committee to take place on one afternoon in early
July. This will be followed by a final interview for the two top candidates seven days

later before the entire Board at which the candidates will be asked to make a
presentation lasting no more than 20 minutes about their vision for ensuring the
financial and artistic success of the Fringe, followed by questions from the Board for a
maximum of 40 minutes. The Board will then discuss both candidates in depth and
make the selection. | will conduct referencing of the shortlisted candidates so that we

are able to make an offer on the same day.
