All you need to know on Brighton Fringe 2024
When is Brighton Fringe 2024 taking place?

Brighton Fringe will take place over the course ofa month from Friday, May 3 to

Sunday, June 2.

Is Brighton Fringe 2024 free?

Some events for Brighton Fringe are free to attend but plenty will also require
you to purchase a ticket.

What events will be taking place at Brighton Fringe 2024?

A huge mixture of events will be available to watch at Brighton Fringe, from
stand-up comedy to musical performances, art exhibitions, workshops and
stage shows.
A full calendar of events for Brighton Fringe 2024 can be found on their website

here.
