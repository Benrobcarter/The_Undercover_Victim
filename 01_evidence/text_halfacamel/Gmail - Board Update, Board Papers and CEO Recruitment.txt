Thank you for this update, which prompts me to raise my concerns about how the recruitment process for our new CEO is
being managed.

| have received no information about this process that as a trustee | might reasonably be expected to know. Specifically:

e

Which trustees were involved in drafting the job description and person specification?

e

Which trustees are involved in shortlisting, who comprises the interview panel, and who was involved in drafting

e

What are the stages of this process e.g. will there be first and second round interviews? Will the staff team have an

interview questions?
opportunity to be involved or input into candidate selection e.g. one or more of the team sitting on the interview panel
and/or a chemistry meeting between the team and shortlisted/final round candidates.
e

By whom and how was all of this decided?

e

And will the rest of the board have any involvement in or be consulted at all on the final candidate selection, prior to
their appointment?

I’m also slightly concerned about the appropriateness of sharing the list of applicants to date (| understand-- only from
seeking out the published advert-- that the vacancy closes this Friday) and then inviting trustees to share personal opinions
on candidates. We surely would want to avoid unfairly prejudicing the selection panel, particularly in advance of shortlisting
applicants fairly against the published person specification criteria.

If it were to transpire - indeed even if there was a suggestion - that the selection process for a new Fringe CEO has not been
entirely fair, thorough and transparent, we would all as trustees be held responsible and accountable.
| hope you can appreciate my concerns and | look forward to gaining more understanding of how this process is being led.
